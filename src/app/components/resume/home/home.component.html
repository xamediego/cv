Experience

Sogeti

Software Engineer

During my graduation internship at Sogeti, I designed and implemented a modern containerized architecture leveraging Java WebAssembly (TeaVM) and Spinkube. I built a registration platform using Kotlin for the backend and TeaVM/HTML/CSS for the frontend, deploying it with Helm on Kubernetes (EKS). The project demonstrated the feasibility of Java-based SPA architectures using TeaVM and WASI runtimes, overcoming limitations such as missing reflection and missing standard and external libraries. I also implemented a custom SPA router, Shadow DOM-based component system, and dynamic JSON parsing logic.

Java • WebAssembly • TeaVM • Kubernetes • Spinkube • Kotlin

Cohelion

Software Development Intern

During my internship at Cohelion, I contributed to the development of a DevOps tool for visualizing and managing dependencies across customer-specific Git repositories and submodules. I focused on data transformation pipelines, backend architecture redesign using SOLID principles, and created intuitive dashboard features with Angular and .NET Core. My work included recursive algorithms for mapping Git trees, visual dependency graphs, and interactive UI components for version control operations.

Angular • .NET Core • Git • SOLID • Dependency Mapping • UI/UX • Software Architecture • TypeScript

Personal

Travel and Living in Japan

My time in Japan included a minor study at Kansai University, a three-month stay in Tokyo where I took language classes, and an extended trip around the country. I was fascinated by the blend of ancient culture and futuristic city life. The Japanese approach to minimalism, attention to detail, and respect for nature had a lasting impact on how I think about design and life in general. It also allowed me to gain a decent grasp of the language, which I still use today for hobbies such as reading Japanese books and watching movies.

This experience broadened my worldview and taught me to thrive in diverse environments. I explored cities like Shenzhen and Xiamen, learned Mandarin, and gained a deep understanding of the academic and social dynamics of Chinese life.

Overland Journey from Russia to Japan

One of my most formative experiences was traveling overland from St. Petersburg to Tokyo — crossing through Siberia, Kazakhstan, and Mongolia by train and car. It was a deeply immersive adventure through time zones, cultures, climates, and languages. That journey gave me lasting perspective on resilience, adaptability, and the interconnectedness of people and technology across borders.
